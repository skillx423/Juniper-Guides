Mining Guide

Overview

  • Mining is fairly stright forward.
  • Click Rock, Get xp.

Fastest XP Methods

  • Iron power mining to coal is pretty fast.
  • Motherload mine prospecter outfit + Coal bag is handy.

    • AFK / Chill Methods

    Motherload mine is pretty chill, also Mining guild coal is close to a bank.

    Level Milestones

    • Level 1–15: Copper + Tin
    • Level 15–40: Iron Ore
    • Level 40–99: Motherload Mine
    • Level 40–70: (Optional)Mining coal for a few levels will supply your smithing.

    Gear Recommendations

    Prospecter outfit + Your best pickaxe. Coal bag if you have it.

    Tips & Tricks

    • Prospecter outfit is handy
    • The Coal bag is nice for power mining
