Herblore Guide

Overview

  • Herblore is pretty quick and fairly afk.
  • Graceful can be bought from Grace in the rogues den in burthrope bar cellar.

Fastest XP Methods

  • Super combat potions are good to collect for divines for bossing.
  • I would reccomend making mostly super restores, brews, super combats and divine combats.

    • AFK / Chill Methods

    Herblore is pretty chill as is.

    Level Milestones

    • Level 1–12: Attack Potions - Guam + eye of newt
    • Level 12–30: Strength Potions - Tarromin + Limpwurt root
    • Level 30–38: Defence Potions - Ranarr + white berries
    • Level 38–45: Prayer Potions - Ranarr + Snape grass
    • Level 45–55: Super Attack Potions - Irit + eye of newt
    • Level 55–63: Super Strength Potions - Kwuarm + Limpwurt root
    • Level 63–66: Super Restore Potions - Snapdragon + red spiders' eggs
    • Level 66–72: Super Defence Potions - Cadantine + White berries
    • Level 72–81: Ranging Potions - Dwarf weed + Wine of Zamorak
    • Level 81–90: Saradomin Brew Potions - Toadflax + Crushed bird nest
    • Level 90–99: Super Combat Potions - Torstol + Super Attack, Strength and Defence Potions

    Gear Recommendations

    Fasionscape is key.
