Level Milestones

  • Level 1–20: Draynor Village Rooftop Course
  • Level 20–30: Al kharid Rooftop Course
  • Level 30–40: Varrock Rooftop Course
  • Level 40–50: Canifis Rooftop Course
  • Level 50–60: Falador Rooftop Course
  • Level 60–75: Seers' Rooftop Course
  • Level 75–80: Prifddinas Course
  • Level 80–90: Rellekka Rooftop Course
  • Level 90–99: Ardougne Rooftop Course

The orb at home has all the agility training spots.
