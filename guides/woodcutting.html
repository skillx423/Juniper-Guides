    Level Milestones

    • Level 1–15: Tree
    • Level 15–30: Oak Trees
    • Level 30–35: Willow Trees
    • Level 35–45: Teak Trees (Prif north east corner)
    • Level 45–50: Maple Trees
    • Level 50–60: Mahogany Trees (Prif north east corner)
    • Level 60–75: Yew Trees
    • Level 75–90: Magic Trees
    • Level 90–99: Redwood Trees
